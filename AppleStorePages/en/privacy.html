Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect through the Nemoris app, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. It applies to app users and visitors to our App Store pages.

1. Data Controller

Controller: Nemoris
Contact: ledger-nest@hedwin.fr

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, login identifiers (if you create an account).
  • Financial data: transactions you import (CSV or export files), stored locally or, if you enable sync, encrypted on our servers.
  • Usage data: anonymized analytics about app usage (events, logs) to improve the service.
  • Technical data: anonymized device identifiers, OS version, diagnostics for errors.

3. Purposes of Processing

We use data to:

  • Provide and maintain app features (sync, backup, restore).
  • Manage in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Improve the product via anonymized analytics.
  • Respond to support requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share some data with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics) only for the purposes above.
  • Authorities when required by law.

5. Storage and Security

Data is protected by standard technical and organizational measures (encryption at rest and in transit for server data). You can request deletion of your data by contacting the address above.

6. International Transfers

If data is transferred outside the EU, we put appropriate safeguards in place (standard contractual clauses, etc.).

7. Your Rights

Access, rectification, deletion, portability, restriction, and objection. To exercise these rights: ledger-nest@hedwin.fr.

8. Children

The app is not intended for persons under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Web pages related to the app may use cookies/analytics. See our site notices if applicable.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date indicates the current version.

Contact: ledger-nest@hedwin.fr
