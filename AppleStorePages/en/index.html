Nemoris — Direction de marque

Nemoris is not "a budgeting app". It is a personal, private, calm and durable financial space. Your finances should belong to you.

What Nemoris stands for

  • Private by design — your data stays under your control
  • Offline‑first: no cloud imposed, no required server dependency
  • No tracking, no third‑party data harvesting
  • Focus on personal financial sovereignty
